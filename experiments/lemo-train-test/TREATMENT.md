# "The Unscheduled Train" — Lemo-Opuscar evaluation film

**Purpose:** a 10-second test of Lemo-Opuscar's real anime output for the AETHER project.
**Style:** 80s Cel Anime (`cel-anime-80s`). Its STYLE.md is fixed: flat two-tone cels, airbrushed plate,
backlit light, characters on twos / acting on threes, camera on ones, tape + CRT playback.
**Format:** 1920×1080, 24 fps, 10.0 s (240 frames). No voice, no subtitles: the scene is wordless
(a Hindi line was considered and dropped: 10 s is too short for a line to add more than it costs).
**Image generator:** none (Codex CLI not installed; it would need the user's paid account). Everything is drawn by code.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **One oner.** A slow crane from the sunset sky down to the platform while the train arrives behind her.
   Elegant, but in 10 s it spends most of the time on the sky and the character stays small.
2. **Approach → pass → reveal (4 shots).** Wide: a light appears on the far curve. Low side angle: the train
   sweeps in behind her and its wind hits her hair and scarf. Close: window light bands slide across her
   face as it slows. Over the shoulder: it stops, a door opens, warm light spills out. Each shot shows one
   thing the test must prove (environment, train motion, hair/cloth, light + acting).
3. **Train POV.** We ride in on the train and see her grow on the platform. Strong motion, but no
   character acting and no secondary motion to judge.

**Chosen: 2.** It is the only structure that tests every requirement (environment, convincing train motion,
hair and clothing, camera, lighting, an emotional change) inside 10 s.

## 2. Logline and arc

A traveller waits alone on a hillside platform at sunset; a train that isn't on any timetable glides in,
and its open door fills her face with gold.
Setup (waiting, calm) → turn (the train's wind and light reach her) → ending (the door opens; she looks
up, decided, at the light).

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from:** late-80s Japanese OVA/TV cel work in general — painted dusk plates against flat cels,
  backlit window light, held-cel drama with sliding light bands, speed-line passes; Japanese rural-line
  station photography (concrete platform edge, tactile strip, catenary poles, a small shelter).
- **Do not take:** any specific film's train, station, characters, character designs, shots, logos or
  music. No water railway, no specific famous station. The train design, livery and the girl are original.

## 6. Cue map

90 BPM (beat = 0.6667 s = 16 frames), 15 beats. Bars of 4 at 0.0 / 2.67 / 5.33 / 8.0 s.
Cuts at 3.0 (horn, off-grid on purpose: the train interrupts), 6.0 (beat 9), 8.0 (bar 4 downbeat).
Score: city-pop/synth palette from the style — FM electric-piano dusk chord bed, then a synth-brass swell
on the door opening (8.4), FM bell sparkle on the light spill (8.8), one beat of near-silence at 7.6–8.0
before the door (the turn).
