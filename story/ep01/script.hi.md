### *Ek Nayi Shuruaat* · *A New Beginning*

## Canon guardrails for this episode

| Rule | How this script follows it |
|---|---|
| Aether, previous-life age 26 | Stated in narration: "छब्बीस साल..." |
| Brown hair, ocean-blue eyes | Visual specs in `art/characters/aether/design-spec.md`. The eyes get a hero ECU (sh050) |
| Intelligent, funny, curious, emotional | Curious: looks around, pats his chest. Funny: "...ऑफ़िस तो नहीं है।" and "मैं... बच्चा बन गया?!". Emotional: "माँ...", tears |
| Keeps previous-life memories | His adult inner voice speaks from the baby (sh160) |
| **Creation Authority stays hidden** | **No hint anywhere.** No glow, symbol, unusual power, or special reaction from Elaria about his abilities. Her golden runes are *her* Divine Knowledge, not his power |
| Elaria: Supreme Goddess, age/origin unknown | Not explained. No backstory dialogue |
| Elaria: identifies souls through Divine Knowledge | sh100: runes turn in her irises as she reads his soul |
| Elaria's first line | Exactly: "आखिरकार... तुम यहाँ पहुँच ही गए।" |
| Astral Nexus | Galaxies, stars, floating islands, celestial structures. Blue / violet / silver / gold |

**Minor creative details added** (can be changed without affecting canon):
- The previous life is shown as a rainy city night after work, with three
  missed calls from his mother that he never returns. This is the regret.
- How he dies is **not shown**: only a blinding white light and silence.
  There is no vehicle, impact or injury on screen.
- The new-world mother and room are kept in soft focus and unrevealed, so
  later episodes keep design freedom.

divine voice. All lines are understandable to a general Hindi audience.

## SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN · ब्रह्मांड (THE COSMOS) · 0.00–4.00 s

**SH010 · 0.00 (f0) – 4.00 (f96)**

*Total darkness. A single star ignites in the centre, first a pinprick and
then a warm flare. We drift slowly toward it through layers of dust and
faint nebula.*

> **AETHER (V.O.)** *(soft, reflective)* · 0.50–3.92 s
> कहते हैं... हर अंत, एक नई शुरुआत होता है।
> *Kehte hain... har ant, ek nayi shuruaat hota hai.*
> **EN:** "They say... every ending is a new beginning."

---

## SCENE 2 — PREVIOUS LIFE · शहर, बारिश की रात (CITY, RAINY NIGHT) · 4.00–11.00 s

**SH020 · 4.00 (f96) – 7.00 (f168)**

*High wide shot. Rain over a neon-lit city street. A sea of umbrellas moves
one way. One figure walks the other way without an umbrella: **AETHER (26)**.
His white office shirt is soaked, his tie loosened, an ID lanyard swings. His
shoulders sag.*

> **AETHER (V.O.)** *(tired, almost amused at himself)* · 4.33–6.67 s
> छब्बीस साल... बस भागता रहा।
> *Chhabbees saal... bas bhaagta raha.*
> **EN:** "Twenty-six years... I just kept running."

**SH030 · 7.00 (f168) – 9.50 (f228)**

*Insert: his phone in his wet hand. Raindrops on the screen:*
**"माँ — 3 मिस्ड कॉल"**. *His thumb hovers over CALL... hesitates... then
presses the lock button. The screen goes dark and reflects his face.*

> **AETHER (V.O.)** *(quiet, heavy)* · 7.17–9.25 s
> जो कहना था... वो कभी कहा ही नहीं।
> *Jo kehna tha... woh kabhi kaha hi nahin.*
> **EN:** "What I needed to say... I never said."

**SH040 · 9.50 (f228) – 11.00 (f264)**

*He looks up. A **blinding white light** floods in from the right. His eyes
widen. The rain **freezes** mid-air. All sound cuts out. White.*

---

## SCENE 3 — AWAKENING · एस्ट्रल नेक्सस (ASTRAL NEXUS) · 11.00–17.00 s

**SH050 · 11.00 (f264) – 13.00 (f312)**

*The white fades into deep violet. Extreme close-up of a closed eye. The
lashes tremble, and the eye opens: an **ocean-blue** iris reflecting entire
galaxies. One slow blink.*

**SH060 · 13.00 (f312) – 17.00 (f408)**

*The camera cranes up. Aether lies on a floating crystal platform and sits
up. Around him is the **ASTRAL NEXUS**: floating islands trailing waterfalls
of light, slow-turning galaxies, silver arches drifting in a blue-violet
sky. He looks left, then right, and pats his chest. He's dry and no longer
hurt.*

> **AETHER** *(breathless, curious)* · 13.92–15.42 s
> ये... कहाँ आ गया मैं?
> *Ye... kahaan aa gaya main?*
> **EN:** "Where... am I?"

*A beat. He takes in the impossible view.*

> **AETHER** *(deadpan, under his breath)* · 15.75–16.83 s
> ...ऑफ़िस तो नहीं है।
> *...Office toh nahin hai.*
> **EN:** "...Definitely not the office."

---

## SCENE 4 — ELARIA · 17.00–23.00 s

**SH070 · 17.00 (f408) – 19.00 (f456)**

*Golden particles drift past his face. One touches his cheek, warm. He turns
slowly to look over his shoulder. Behind him the particles begin to
spiral.*

**SH080 · 19.00 (f456) – 23.00 (f552)**

*Low angle. The golden spiral gathers into a woman's shape: first bare feet
above the crystal, then flowing white-and-gold robes, then long
**silver-white hair** unfurling as if underwater. **ELARIA** opens her eyes:
**gold**. A faint, knowing smile.*

> **ELARIA** *(calm, warm, as if she had waited a long time)* · 20.42–22.92 s
> आखिरकार... तुम यहाँ पहुँच ही गए।
> *Aakhirkaar... tum yahaan pahunch hi gaye.*
> **EN:** "At last... you've finally arrived."

---

## SCENE 5 — THE TRUTH · 23.00–31.00 s

**SH090 · 23.00 (f552) – 25.00 (f600)**

*Aether starts with a confused half-smile, which fades as the meaning sinks
in.*

> **AETHER** *(half-laugh, then slowing)* · 23.17–24.92 s
> पहुँच गया...? मतलब... मैं...
> *Pahunch gaya...? Matlab... main...*
> **EN:** "Arrived...? You mean... I'm..."

**SH100 · 25.00 (f600) – 28.50 (f684)**

*Close on Elaria. A ring of fine **golden runes** turns inside her irises:
**Divine Knowledge**, reading his soul. The glow fades and her eyes soften.*

> **ELARIA** *(gentle, unhurried, a small nod on "हाँ")* · 25.25–28.42 s
> हाँ। तुम्हारा पिछला जीवन... समाप्त हो चुका है।
> *Haan. Tumhaara pichhla jeevan... samaapt ho chuka hai.*
> **EN:** "Yes. Your previous life... has ended."

**SH110 · 28.50 (f684) – 31.00 (f744)**

*Extreme close-up on Aether. His eyes tremble, a tear wells and falls, and
his lips part. The colour around him drains slightly.*

> **AETHER** *(a broken whisper)* · 29.83–30.67 s
> माँ...
> *Maa...*
> **EN:** "Mom..."

---

## SCENE 6 — ANOTHER CHANCE · 31.00–35.50 s

**SH120 · 31.00 (f744) – 35.50 (f852)**

*Over Aether's shoulder. Elaria glides one step closer, bends slightly and
offers an open hand. The particles around her brighten.*

> **ELARIA** *(soft, curious, no pressure)* · 31.58–35.00 s
> अगर तुम्हें एक और मौका मिले... तो क्या करोगे?
> *Agar tumhein ek aur mauka mile... toh kya karoge?*
> **EN:** "If you were given one more chance... what would you do?"

---

## SCENE 7 — ACCEPTANCE · 35.50–40.00 s

**SH130 · 35.50 (f852) – 40.00 (f960)**

*Aether wipes the tear with his wrist, lets out a short breath of a laugh,
and grins: his old self again. The grin turns into resolve. Warm light
returns to his face.*

> **AETHER** *(steady, smiling through emotion)* · 36.67–39.08 s
> इस बार... हर पल खुलकर जिऊँगा।
> *Is baar... har pal khulkar jiyoonga.*
> **EN:** "This time... I'll live every moment to the fullest."

*He takes her hand.*

---

## SCENE 8 — THE SENDING · 40.00–44.50 s

**SH140 · 40.00 (f960) – 43.00 (f1032)**

*Wide two-shot. Elaria raises her free palm. A **golden magic circle** opens
beneath Aether, and columns of light rise. He looks at her and nods. He
dissolves into light from his feet upward.*

> **ELARIA** *(serene, a blessing)* · 40.25–42.67 s
> तो जाओ... अपनी नई शुरुआत की ओर।
> *Toh jaao... apni nayi shuruaat ki or.*
> **EN:** "Then go... toward your new beginning."

**SH150 · 43.00 (f1032) – 44.50 (f1068)**

*His point of view as a streak of light rushing through stars and galaxies.
Speed lines stretch the stars. White.*

---

## SCENE 9 — BORN AGAIN · नया संसार (A NEW WORLD) · 44.50–50.00 s

**SH160 · 44.50 (f1068) – 47.50 (f1140)**

*Blurry white slowly comes into soft focus: warm morning light, a wooden
window and dust in a sunbeam. We **don't** see the new mother clearly; she
is a gentle warm blur. Close on a **tiny hand** grasping at air. A **baby's
ocean-blue eyes** open, blink and stare.*

> **AETHER (INNER VOICE, adult)** *(stunned → comic panic)* · 45.83–47.42 s
> ...रुको। मैं... बच्चा बन गया?!
> *...Ruko. Main... bachcha ban gaya?!*
> **EN:** "...Wait. I'm... a baby?!"

**SH170 · 47.50 (f1140) – 50.00 (f1200)**

*The camera slowly pulls back. The baby blinks twice, then twitches his nose
in a tiny sneeze. The title card shines in:*

**एक नई शुरुआत**
*Ek Nayi Shuruaat*

*END OF EPISODE 1.*
