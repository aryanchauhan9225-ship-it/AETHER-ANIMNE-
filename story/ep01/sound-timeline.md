## 1. Sound concept

- **Two worlds, two sounds.** The previous life is **real and
  mechanical**: rain, traffic hum and phone buzz, with very little music.
  The Astral Nexus is **musical and weightless**: pads, choir, harp and
  bells, with almost no realistic sound.
- **Silence is a tool.** The white-out (sh040) cuts everything to total
  silence for 16 frames. That's the "death", told by sound.
- **Musical identity:** an anime fantasy orchestral palette (strings, harp,
  celesta, soft choir, piano) with **one Indian colour**: a **bansuri**
  (bamboo flute) carrying Aether's theme. It ties the Hindi voice to the
  score.
- **Elaria's motif:** celesta + harp arpeggio + wordless female vocalise
  (an "aah" vowel, no lyrics), 4 notes rising.
- **Aether's motif:** a simple 5-note bansuri phrase, first sad (SC2 / SC5)
  and then hopeful (SC7, SC9).

## 5. Mix plan (FFmpeg-based, at the mix stage)

| Bus | Target |
|---|---|
| Dialogue | Peaks around −6 dBFS before the master, ~10 dB above the music |
| Music | Ducked ~8–9 dB under dialogue, with a 6-frame fade in and out |
| Ambience | Ducked ~3–6 dB under dialogue |
| SFX | Placed by ear. Silences (cues 9, 25) are absolute |
| **Master** | **−14 LUFS integrated**, true peak ≤ −1.0 dBTP, two-pass `loudnorm` |

Stems to keep (for later dubbing or subtitled versions): `dialogue.wav`,
`music.wav`, `ambience.wav`, `sfx.wav`, `mix.wav` in `audio/ep01/mix/`.
