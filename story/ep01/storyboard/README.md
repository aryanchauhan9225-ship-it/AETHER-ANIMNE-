and character placement only. They are not final art and show no real acting. Hybrid workflow (option C):
artists replace each panel with a drawn Krita panel **of the same id** (export `<id>.png` next to the SVG;
the animatic build prefers a PNG over the SVG when both exist).

Timing source: `story/ep01/shotlist.yaml` → `panels:`. Regenerate with
`python3 scripts/animatic/gen_panels.py`; do not hand-edit the SVGs.
