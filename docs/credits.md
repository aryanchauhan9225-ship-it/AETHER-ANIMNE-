## 1. Technique references (inspiration, no code copied)

### Lemo-Opuscar, by Lemomo (LemoLab)

- Source: <https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar>
- Reviewed: 2026-11-12 (read-only). **Not installed.**
- What we took: **ideas only**, from the *80s Cel Anime* style
  (`styles/cel-anime-80s/STYLE.md`). These are a separate glow layer,
  masking characters out of the glow, halation, rim light from an offset
  shape, light-band sweeps over held drawings, impact frames, per-lighting
  colour models, and order-of-operations pitfalls. They are written up in
  our own words in `docs/style-guide.md` §2.5 and §4.1.
- Code copied: **none.** Our FX/Grade modules are written from scratch.
- Licences, as stated in its `LICENSE` file:
  - Code: MIT License. Copyright (c) 2026 Lemomo (LemoLab).
  - Docs and style prompts: CC BY-NC 4.0, plus an additional permission
    letting users follow and adapt the docs to make their own films,
    commercial films included.

  licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0
  (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/>), changed.
